Make GTA 6 as a 3D game that runs in my web browser. It should feel like a modern open-world crime game in the style of GTA 6, as close as you can get it, and like a finished game, not a prototype. Use your own original city, characters, cars, story and art: no Rockstar names, logos, maps, characters or files.

The complete game:
- Title screen with a menu: Start, Options, Quit.
- A big sunny Florida-style coastal city: streets with lanes and intersections, traffic lights, sidewalks, palm trees, beaches and water, a downtown with tall buildings, neighborhoods, shops, a highway and a bridge.
- Third-person view of my character. WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to sprint, Space to jump.
- Cars driving around the city with traffic AI. Press F to steal and enter any car, drive it with a chase camera, crash into things with damage, exit again.
- Pedestrians walking the sidewalks who react and run when there is danger.
- Weapons: punch, a pistol and a rifle with a crosshair, aiming, shooting, ammo and reloading.
- A wanted level with stars: crimes bring police cars that chase me, more stars bring more police, and hiding long enough clears it.
- Health and armor, death and respawn at a hospital.
- A minimap with roads, my position and missions, and a full map screen.
- At least three missions with markers, objectives and rewards, and money I earn and spend.
- A day and night cycle with a sun, street lights and car headlights at night.
- A radio in cars with a few stations of music made in code.
- Sounds made in code: engines, horns, gunshots, sirens, footsteps, crashes.
- Saving and loading progress in the browser.
- Options: graphics quality, field of view, mouse sensitivity, volume.

What you have to work with:
- `node ../../../tools/shot.js <out.png> [query] [wait_ms]` takes a 1280x720 screenshot of your index.html running in Chrome. Make index.html support `?autoplay=1` (skip the menus, start the game, and let a computer player walk around, steal a car, drive through the city and cause some chaos) plus query options to screenshot each menu, driving, a police chase, night time and the map, so you can see the real game. Look at your screenshots. Use them often, check everything the way a player would see it, and fix anything that looks wrong, empty or basic.
- Libraries load from cdnjs or jsdelivr. Use a recent three.js.
- The game is served over http from this folder.
